I am **Hadi Tabealhojeh**, an Assistant Professor at the <a href="https://www.ui.ac.ir/">University of Isfahan</a> in Iran and an AI/ML consultant working with industry teams. My work connects machine-learning research with practical AI systems. You can find more about my professional work on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/haditabe">LinkedIn</a>, my research publications on <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dd8X_HgAAAAJ&amp;hl=fa">Google Scholar</a>, and selected code and projects on <a href="https://github.com/haditabealhojeh">GitHub</a>.

I completed my PhD in **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics** at the University of Isfahan (2019–2024). My doctoral research focused on **meta-learning in Riemannian spaces**, particularly optimization under geometric constraints and learning from limited data. This research also involved international academic collaborations with <a href="https://www.monash.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Monash University</a>, including collaboration with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=3ZK6G0AAAAAJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Professor Mehrtash Harandi</a>; the <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/labs/cvlab/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Computer Vision Laboratory (CVLab) at EPFL</a>; and <a href="https://www.grenoble-inp.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Professor Jocelyn Chanussot</a> and the <a href="https://www.gipsa-lab.grenoble-inp.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GIPSA-lab</a>.

My broader work spans meta-learning, few-shot learning, Riemannian geometry, manifold learning and optimization, deep learning, and multimodal learning, as well as applied AI, model deployment, and machine-learning engineering.
