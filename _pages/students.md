I am interested in working with motivated students on research and applied projects in machine learning, meta-learning, geometric deep learning, manifold optimization, multimodal learning, and AI systems.

This page is intended to introduce undergraduate, master's, and PhD students whose research I supervise or collaborate with. I will add individual profiles after confirming each student's preferred name, research topic, and consent to be featured publicly.

## Student research profiles

No individual student profiles are published here yet. When profiles are added, each entry can include:

- **Name and degree level** — undergraduate, master's, or PhD.
- **Research topic** — a short description of the student's work.
- **Project or publication** — where publicly available.
- **Personal webpage or academic profile** — optional, and included with the student's permission.

## Prospective students

If you are interested in research collaboration or a student project, please get in touch with a short introduction, your research interests, and links to relevant academic or project work.
